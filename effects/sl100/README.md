# SL-100

SUPER LEAD 100: an original three-channel 100 W lead head model inspired by
the Soldano SLO-100. MODE selects CLEAN, CLEAN BRT, CRUNCH, CRUNCH BRT or LEAD
(BRT = the bright cap, Clean and Crunch only); GAIN, BASS, MID, TREBLE,
PRESENCE, MASTER and LEVEL as on the other Frost amp heads. The power amp's
low resonance (DEPTH) is voiced per channel. Own overlay module; no captures,
cabinet or workspace: add an IR block after it. Host tested
(tests/test_sl100.py); physical sound/CPU qualification pending.
Not a measured match, manufacturer implementation or endorsement.
